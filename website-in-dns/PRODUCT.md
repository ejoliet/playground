# Website in DNS

A research app for curious developers: reconstruct an entire interactive document from real DNS TXT records, then inspect the costs and limits. Content includes a geometric image gallery, searchable technical notes, a memory game, and a resolver inspector. No external fonts, image URLs, analytics, or content API. A local bootstrap is necessary because browsers do not navigate directly to TXT records.

All edits stay in this folder. Live results and local fixtures must be clearly distinguished. DNS publishing is restricted to an isolated research namespace.
